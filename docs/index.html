Why it matters

Every capable agent introduces the same failure mode: the ask drifts. Soft agreements, polite “done,” and clever detours that weren’t what you meant. More model power doesn’t fix that — it amplifies it.

  • The ask is the asset. If intent isn’t recorded, nothing downstream can be honest.
  • Enforcement beats vibes. Constraints belong next to the work, not in a forgotten prompt.
  • Verification closes the loop. Did the outcome match the charter — yes, no, or drifted?

1 · Record

Capture the charter: goal, musts, must-nots, success checks. Plain language. Durable. Shareable with humans and agents.

2 · Enforce

Keep the charter in the loop while work runs. Scope, constraints, and stop conditions stay visible — not optional flavor text.

3 · Verify

Check outcomes against the original ask. Pass, fail, or drift — with a trail you can audit. Verification under the hood; intent on the surface.
